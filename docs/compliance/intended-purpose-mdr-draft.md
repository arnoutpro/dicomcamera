# Intended purpose (MDR draft — not legal advice)

**Product (working name):** DICOM Camera (Android)

**Intended purpose:**  
Software application for healthcare professionals to capture photographic and video clinical documentation at the point of care, associate the media with the correct patient and order/exam context, encode it as DICOM objects, and transmit it to a connected Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS) or Vendor Neutral Archive (VNA) for inclusion in the patient imaging record.

**Not intended for:**  
- Autonomous diagnosis, triage, or clinical decision-making
- Replacement of diagnostic modalities (CT/MR/US/XA, etc.)
- Long-term on-device archiving of health data
- Consumer / patient self-use outside professional care settings

**Users:** Trained healthcare professionals under the responsibility of a zorginstelling.

**Environment:** Managed Android devices on hospital networks (typically MDM-enrolled).

**Regulatory note:** Classification under EU MDR (including possible Rule 11 considerations) must be confirmed with qualified regulatory counsel before CE marking / placing on the market. This draft exists to drive architecture and QMS readiness, not as a final clinical evaluation.
